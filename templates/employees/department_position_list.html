How Departments, Positions & Operating Dates Work Together

  • Standard Departments: Barn, Kitchen, Housekeeping, Maintenance, Fishing, Office, and Bar represent the core ranch operations. You can add extra custom departments at any time.
  • Leadership Roles: Roles marked as Department Head / Boss (such as Head Wrangler, Corral Boss, Barn Boss in the Barn, or Head Chef in the Kitchen) indicate supervisory and key leadership positions.
  • Operating Season Staffing Headcounts: In the Operating Dates section, you can set the exact target headcount (e.g. 10 Wranglers, 2 Wrangler/Packers, 1 Barn Boss) for any season.
  • Auto-Fill Sync: When saving or updating operating season targets, you can click "Auto-Fill Schedule" to automatically generate placeholder positions for the season. Similarly, any existing schedule entries will automatically sync back into the season targets.
