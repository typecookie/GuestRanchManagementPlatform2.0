How Operating Dates Work

  • Cabin Grid View: The cabin grid highlights open operating weeks and clearly indicates closed / off-season weeks. You can also use the "Filter Closed Weeks" toggle to display only open weeks.
  • Default Landing: When opening the Cabin Grid or dashboard, the system automatically focuses on the active operating season (June to September by default) rather than closed winter months.
  • Reservation List: You can filter reservations specifically by active operating seasons or view off-season reservations.
  • Defaults: If no explicit season records are in the database, the system automatically uses the standard ranch schedule (June 1 to September 30).
